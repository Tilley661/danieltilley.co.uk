# Privacy Notice – Personal Music Assistant Alexa Integration **Last updated:** 18 October 2026 This application is a privately operated integration connecting Amazon Alexa devices with a self-hosted Music Assistant server. **Purpose** The integration allows authorised users to control music playback through compatible Amazon Alexa devices. **Information processed** The integration may process Amazon account authorisation information, device identifiers, playback commands and music streaming requests as necessary to provide its functionality. **Data storage and sharing** The integration is hosted on privately managed infrastructure. Information is processed only as required to operate the integration. Amazon and any configured music services may independently process information according to their own privacy policies. **Security** Access is restricted to authorised users, and public connections use HTTPS. **Contact** For privacy-related enquiries, contact the application administrator through the contact details provided on this website.
